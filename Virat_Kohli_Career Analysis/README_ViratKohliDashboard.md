
# 🏏 Virat Kohli Score Dashboard (Power BI)

This interactive **Power BI** dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of **Virat Kohli's cricket career**, offering insights into his performance over the years across different opponents, formats, and key metrics.

## 📊 Project Overview
The dashboard highlights Virat Kohli’s career statistics, allowing users to explore his runs, centuries, half-centuries, and performance trends. It enables fans, analysts, and cricket enthusiasts to better understand the journey of one of the greatest cricketers in modern times.

## 🔑 Key Insights & Features
- **Total Runs:** 22K+ career runs with a highest score of **254**.
- **Milestones:** 115 half-centuries (50s) and 77 centuries (100s).
- **Batting Average:** Career average of **47.62**.
- **Runs by Opponent:** Detailed breakdown against teams like Australia, England, West Indies, Sri Lanka, and South Africa.
- **Matches by Opponent:** Number of matches played against each major cricketing nation.
- **Runs Over the Years:** Performance trend from 2008 to 2020 showing consistency and peak years.
- **Career Span:** Covers 15 years of international cricket.
